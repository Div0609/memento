Packaging concept · Delegate memento 

Packaging that is never put away. The case is the shrine.

A lacquered cabinet with twin doors. Closed, it travels and hands over as a sealed oxblood-and-bronze object. Opened, the doors swing back, and the memento stands on its bronze rail with its own concealed light already on, exactly as it was photographed. It goes from the ceremony straight onto a desk or console, already displayed.

Handed over

Sealed. Oxblood lacquer, one bronze seam where the doors meet, a plaque on the plinth. Nothing else.

Opened

Doors to 112°. The frame's own light is waiting behind them. The certificate drawer sits below the relief.

The memento it holds

13 × 11 × 3 in · Italian slate relief, ebonised frame, twin brass fillets. The cabinet answers the black frame with oxblood, and the brass with aged bronze.

What makes it new

01

Vertical, not flat. Every gift box lies down and gets stored. This one stands, so the recipient never has to decide where the frame goes. It is already placed.

02

No light of its own. The memento is already lit by a single focus bulb inside its frame, so the cabinet adds nothing that competes. It stays dark and quiet around the relief; a reed switch in the door seam wakes the frame's light as the doors part.

03

Doors, so there is a ritual. Two hands, two bronze pulls, a slow reveal from the centre outward. Opening becomes part of the presentation on stage.

04

A drawer for the words. The plinth holds a silk-lined drawer for the certificate and a signed letter on cotton paper, kept with the object instead of in a folder.

Construction

Carcass

14 mm hardwood-faced MDF, mitred corners, eight-coat oxblood lacquer. Outside 43.8 × 42.3 × 18.2 cm. Approx. 6.5 kg with the frame.

Doors

16 mm, three bronze barrel hinges each, soft-close to 112°. Padded oxblood leather inside. Bronze-foil double rule on the face; 2 mm bronze strip on the meeting edge. Concealed magnetic catch.

Chamber

Champagne silk on all faces. Frame leans back 6° against the silk back and rests on an 8 mm bronze rail, 30 mm up. Two bronze contact pads on the floor feed its bulb. Two hidden neodymium pads hold it in transit.

Power

The frame's own light draws from a USB-C cell in the plinth through two bronze contact pads on the rail. No visible cable; the frame lifts off freely.

Plinth and drawer

70 mm plinth with a bronze perimeter line. Push-to-open drawer, silk lined, sized for the A5 cotton certificate and a folded letter. Engraved bronze plaque centred on the drawer front.

Transit

Cabinet ships in a oxblood cotton dust bag inside a fitted foam shipper; the bag stays with the piece as a cover.

Door face

Each door carries the frame's twin-fillet motif in bronze foil: a 2.5 mm outer and 1.5 mm inner rule. The bronze meeting seam reads as a single vertical line when closed and disappears when open.

Emblem and wording go on the plinth plaque only, 110 × 32 mm, engraved and blackened.
