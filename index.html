Packaging concept · Delegate memento

Packaging that is never thrown away. The box is the display.

A lacquered cabinet with twin doors. Closed, it travels and hands over as a sealed oxblood-and-bronze object. Opened, the doors swing back, and the memento stands on its bronze rail, lit by its own concealed bulb, exactly as it was photographed. The same box that protects it in transit is the one it lives in afterwards, on a desk or console.

Opened

Doors to 112°. The frame stands lit by its own bulb. The certificate drawer sits below the relief.

The memento it holds

13 × 11 × 3 in · Italian slate relief, ebonised frame, twin brass fillets. The cabinet answers the black frame with oxblood, and the brass with aged bronze.

What makes it new

01

Packaging you keep. Every gift box lies down and gets stored or binned. This one stands, closes, carries and displays, so nothing is discarded and the frame always has a home.

02

No light of its own. The memento is already lit by a single focus bulb inside its frame, so the cabinet adds nothing that competes. It stays dark and quiet around the relief.

03

Doors, so there is a ritual. Two hands, fingertips in the bronze grooves either side of the seam, a slow reveal from the centre outward. Opening becomes part of the presentation on stage.

04

A drawer for the words. The plinth holds a silk-lined drawer for the certificate and a signed letter on cotton paper, kept with the object instead of in a folder.

Construction

Carcass

9/16 in hardwood-faced MDF, mitred corners, eight-coat oxblood lacquer. Outside 15⅛ × 15⅞ × 5⅞ in; about 12 lb with the frame.

Doors

⅝ in, three bronze barrel hinges each, soft-close to 112°. Padded oxblood leather inside. Bronze-foil double rule on the face; 2 mm bronze strip on the meeting edge; a bronze finger groove beside it on each door. Concealed magnetic catches.

Chamber

Champagne silk on all faces, 5 mm around the frame. Frame leans back 6° against the silk back and rests on an 8 mm bronze rail. Four hidden magnets hold it in transit; it lifts out by hand.

Plinth and drawer

2¾ in plinth with a bronze perimeter line. Drawer opens from a matching bronze finger groove, silk lined, sized for the A5 cotton certificate and a folded letter. Engraved bronze plaque centred on the drawer front, below the groove.

Carrying

A bronze finger groove in the top, matching the doors and drawer, lifts the cabinet; nothing shifts in hand. Ships in an oxblood cotton bag with a strap, inside a fitted foam shipper.

Door face

Each door carries the frame's twin-fillet motif in bronze foil: a 2.5 mm outer and 1.5 mm inner rule. The bronze meeting seam, flanked by the two finger grooves, reads as a single vertical mark when closed and disappears when open.

Emblem and wording go on the plinth plaque only, 4⅜ × 1 in, engraved and blackened.
