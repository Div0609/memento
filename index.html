Packaging concept · Delegate memento

Not the box it arrives in. The one it lives in.

A twin-door cabinet in oxblood lacquer and aged bronze. Closed, it is a sealed object that travels well and hands over with weight. Opened, the doors part on a slow hinge and the memento is already standing on its bronze rail, lit by its own bulb, exactly as it was photographed. The same case that carried it to the ceremony is the one it lives in afterwards, on a desk or a console, for years.

Handed over

Sealed. Deep oxblood lacquer, one vertical bronze seam, three recessed bronze grooves, an engraved plaque. Nothing else on the surface.

Opened

Doors rest at 112°. The relief stands upright in champagne silk, lit from within. Beneath it, the drawer holds the certificate.

The memento it holds

13 × 11 × 3 in · Italian slate relief in an ebonised frame with twin brass fillets. The cabinet takes its cues directly from it: oxblood against the black frame, aged bronze against the brass.

Why it works

01

Packaging that earns its place. Most gift boxes lie flat, get stored, then get thrown away. This one stands upright, closes cleanly, carries comfortably and displays the memento better than any shelf would. Nothing is discarded. The frame always has a home.

02

Restraint around the object. The memento carries its own single focus bulb, so the cabinet adds no light of its own. It stays dark and quiet around the relief, and the slate does the talking.

03

Doors, so there is a ritual. Two hands, fingertips in the bronze grooves either side of the seam, a slow reveal from the centre outward. The act of opening becomes part of the presentation, on stage and every time afterwards.

04

A drawer for the words. The plinth holds a silk-lined drawer for the certificate and a signed letter on cotton paper, so the record stays with the object rather than in a file somewhere else.

Construction

Carcass

9/16 in hardwood-faced MDF, mitred corners, eight-coat oxblood lacquer. Outside 15⅛ × 15⅞ × 5⅞ in; about 12 lb with the frame.

Doors

⅝ in, three bronze barrel hinges each, soft-close to 112°. Padded oxblood leather inside. Bronze-foil double rule on the face; 2 mm bronze strip on the meeting edge; a bronze finger groove beside it on each door. Concealed magnetic catches.

Chamber

Champagne silk on all faces, 5 mm around the frame. Frame leans back 6° against the silk back and rests on an 8 mm bronze rail. Four hidden magnets hold it in transit; it lifts out by hand.

Plinth and drawer

2¾ in plinth with a bronze perimeter line. Drawer opens from a matching bronze finger groove, silk lined, sized for the A5 cotton certificate and a folded letter. Engraved bronze plaque centred on the drawer front, below the groove.

Carrying

A bronze finger groove in the top, matching the doors and drawer, lifts the cabinet; nothing shifts in hand. Ships in an oxblood cotton bag with a strap, inside a fitted foam shipper.

How it opens

Three moves, no parts to find
01

Set it down. It stands on its bottom edge. Fold the handle flush into the top.

02

Open the cover. A thumb in the notch on the top edge; the cover swings down on its bottom hinge. Inside it, the certificate. The relief is already lit.

03

Swing it under. The cover passes beneath the case and lies flat behind it, emblem up. The case settles onto it at twelve degrees and stays.

Handed over

Plain oxblood leather, the State Emblem of India in bronze foil. Bronze only on the hinge line and the handle; the thumb notch sits in the top edge, out of sight.

On display

The relief in its silk surround with nothing in front of it. Behind, the cover lies flat with the emblem facing up. The handle is folded flush.

The memento it holds

13 × 11 × 3 in. Italian slate relief in an ebonised frame with twin brass fillets, lit by its own focus bulb. The case adds no light and nothing that competes.

Why it works

01

Built around the hand. The handle sits on the top edge, centred over the mass, so eleven pounds hang straight and the case rides flat against the leg like a briefcase. Four inches deep, it passes through doors, car seats and security trays without being turned, and it fits an overhead locker.

02

The cover is the stand. Hinged along the bottom front edge, it swings down and under to lie flat behind the case, and the case rests on it. Its footprint is the base; the hinge stop sets the twelve-degree lean. No strut, no plinth, no separate part. The lid that protected the memento in transit is what presents it on the desk.

03

Held, not rattling. The cavity is silk over foam with two millimetres of interference, so the frame presses in and stays. Closed, the cover bears on the frame face through two silk pads near its top edge. Nothing moves in transit, and the frame never has to leave the case to be shown. When it should, two fine bronze thumb grooves in the silk rim let it be lifted straight out and hung on the wall; the case closes empty and keeps the certificate.

04

Leather, because it travels. Full-grain oxblood over a thin birch shell: lighter than lacquer, warm in the hand, forgiving of a knock, better with age. Bronze appears only where it works: the hinge, the handle, the notch, the clips, the emblem.

Construction

Shell

6 mm birch ply, mitred and splined, wrapped in 1.2 mm full-grain leather with turned edges. Outside 14¼ × 12¼ × 4 in closed; about 11 lb with the frame.

Cover

12 mm panel on a full-width bronze piano hinge along the bottom front edge. Swings 270° under the case to lie flat behind; the case rests on it at 12°, set by the hinge stop. Outside: plain leather, State Emblem of India in bronze foil, 90 mm. Inside: silk-lined A5 certificate well under two bronze clips, two silk pads that bear on the frame when closed.

Cavity

Champagne silk over 4 mm foam on all five faces, 2 mm interference on the frame. Frame face sits 2 mm below the rim. Two bronze-lined thumb grooves, 10 × 60 × 16 mm, in the side silk at mid-height, to lift the frame out for wall hanging.

Handle

Bronze D-bail, 8 mm bar, leather-wrapped grip, on two pivots in the top edge. Folds flush into a bronze-lined recess 124 × 70 × 10 mm. Rated 50 lb.

Closure

Four concealed magnets in the rim. One bronze-lined thumb notch, 60 × 8 × 6 mm, in the top edge of the cover. The 8 mm bronze knuckle along the bottom front edge is the only wearing edge, open or closed.

Compared with the cabinet

Cabinet 15⅛ × 15⅞ × 5⅞ in, about 1,410 in³, 12 lb. Case 14¼ × 12¼ × 4 in, about 710 in³, 11 lb. Same frame, same lighting, same palette. The case trades the plinth and drawer for a handle and a cover that is lid, stand and certificate holder in one.
